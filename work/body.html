Our Mission

Through the Tesseract Accelerator, we empower innovative projects with comprehensive support across Advisory, Marketing, Business Development, and Community.

By bringing together visionary projects and experienced leaders, our Track & Funnel method focuses on the projects that receive grants from the LitVM ecosystem.

Four Dimensions of Support

A dedicated Account Executive identifies what your project needs, brings in the right support, and follows up monthly.

01

Advisory

Top-tier guidance from seasoned blockchain experts. Whether it's technical direction or strategic positioning, insights that align with your project's vision.

02

Marketing

Amplify your project's voice through curated appearances at events and Spaces such as QuickSwap's The Aggregated, with a tailored marketing audit and framework.

03

Business Development

Strategic partnerships within our network and the LitVM ecosystem. Collaborate with leading DEXs, DeFi protocols, and other ecosystem projects.

04

Community

A thriving community around your project, with a large-scale campaign, Telegram and Twitter growth, and members who participate, advocate, and spread your vision.
